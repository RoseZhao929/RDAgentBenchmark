\subsection{J.5 Cost-efficiency dichotomy}\label{j.5-cost-efficiency-dichotomy}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Classical / offline} (LIRICAL, VC-RDAgent): \$0 LLM cost on any number
  of cases. LIRICAL on PP-Store achieves R@1 = 0.47 at \$0 cost --- the most
  cost-efficient cell in the entire benchmark. The Phenopacket-Store
  classical lead is therefore also a cost-efficiency result.
\item
  \textbf{DeepSeek V4-Flash} is the cheapest LLM at \$5--10 per cell, but trades
  off accuracy (typically \ensuremath{-}2 to \ensuremath{-}6 pp R@1 vs Gemini Flash on the three
  diagnostic datasets). The gap is largest on RareArena free text; on
  HPO-list inputs the smaller gap may be acceptable.
\item
  \textbf{GPT-5 minimal} is the most expensive per-case (\$0.012--0.05) without a
  consistent accuracy edge (F4 in \Cref{sec-6}). Cost-per-correct-prediction on GPT-5
  is therefore the worst of the four backbones at any N.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{J.6 Reproducibility note}\label{j.6-reproducibility-note}

The receipts CSV is regenerated by \texttt{scripts/regen\_receipts\_and\_figures.py}
and the per-backbone running total by \texttt{scripts/cost\_tracker.py\ -\/-budget\ X}.
Any anonymous reviewer can verify Table J.1 by running these scripts against
our released \texttt{data/round2/phase4a/predictions\_*.jsonl}. The planned cost cap
for the v1 evaluation was \$360; the realised total in Table J.1 is within this
cap. Prices are the frozen evaluation-time prices, not current vendor
quotations.

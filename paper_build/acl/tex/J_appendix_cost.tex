\hypertarget{j.5-cost-efficiency-dichotomy}{%
\subsection{J.5 Cost-efficiency dichotomy}\label{j.5-cost-efficiency-dichotomy}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Classical / offline} (LIRICAL, VC-RDAgent): \$0 LLM cost on any number
  of cases. LIRICAL on PP-Store achieves R@1 = 0.47 at \$0 cost --- the most
  cost-efficient cell in the entire benchmark. F1 (classical \textgreater{} LLM) is also a
  cost-efficiency story.
\item
  \textbf{DeepSeek V4-Flash} is the cheapest LLM at \$5--10 per cell, but trades
  off accuracy (\ensuremath{-}2 to \ensuremath{-}16 pp R@1 vs Gemini Flash). For deployment at scale
  on free-text datasets the \ensuremath{-}16 pp is large enough to recommend Gemini over
  V4-Flash; on HPO-list inputs the \ensuremath{-}5 pp gap may be acceptable.
\item
  \textbf{GPT-5 minimal} is the most expensive per-case (\$0.012--0.05) without a
  consistent accuracy edge (F4 in \Cref{sec-6}). Cost-per-correct-prediction on GPT-5
  is therefore the worst of the four backbones at any N.
\end{itemize}

Any anonymous reviewer can verify Table J.1 by running these scripts against
our released \texttt{data/round2/phase4a/predictions\_*.jsonl}. Cost cap for the v1
evaluation was pre-registered at \$360; the realised total in Table J.1 is
within this cap.

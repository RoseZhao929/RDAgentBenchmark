\begin{abstract}
\section{Abstract}\label{sec-1}

Rare disease diagnostic AI agents have proliferated (8+ systems in
2024-2026), yet no shared benchmark exists; each agent paper evaluates
on an ad-hoc subset, making cross-system claims unverifiable. We
introduce RareAgentBench, an agent-native benchmark spanning five capability pillars (phenotype extraction, phenotype-only DDx, genotype-aware DDx,
family-aware DDx, clinical-communication faithfulness) on a layered
dataset (Phenopacket-Store \emph{n}=10,051; RareBench HF 1,122; RareArena RDS
72,661; post-cutoff PMC OA holdout \emph{n}=200), plus a separately reported
MIMIC-IV structured-EHR probe (956 code-supervised admissions; no clinical
notes). We evaluate 8 agent systems (DeepRare, MDAgents, MedAgents,
AgentClinic, MAI-DxO, RDMA, VC-RDAgent, LIRICAL) against 3 LLM no-scaffolding controls and one classical baseline, with all
hypotheses (H1--H11) and ablations (A1--A12) pre-registered.

\textbf{Key findings}: (1) classical/offline baselines (LIRICAL 0.47,
VC-RDAgent 0.44 R@1) exceed every scaffolded LLM agent on HPO-input datasets (best LLM cell 0.30 on Phenopacket-Store, a 17 pp gap); (2)
multi-agent scaffolding gives only a small, dataset-dependent gain
(\ensuremath{\approx}2--5 pp R@1 over single-LLM controls), not the uniform boost prior
work implies; (3) DeepSeek V4-Flash is \textasciitilde10\ensuremath{\times} cheaper than Gemini Flash
but trades off accuracy (\ensuremath{-}2 to \ensuremath{-}9 pp on structured input, \ensuremath{-}11 to
\ensuremath{-}16 pp on free-text) --- cost-efficient, not quality-equivalent; (4)
GPT-5 with \texttt{reasoning\_effort=minimal} carries the highest cost (\textasciitilde34\ensuremath{\times}
V4-Flash) without a consistent accuracy edge --- competitive on some
scaffolds yet collapsing on dialogue (\ensuremath{-}14 pp on AgentClinic),
illustrating frontier-reasoning models' brittleness under reasoning-disabled regimes; (5) an ORPHA-variant evaluation channel
adds \textasciitilde20 pp R@1 universally --- \emph{not} DeepRare-specific. We release
the harness, canonical case schema, per-agent adapter shims, full
per-cell receipts, and a static-site leaderboard.

\end{abstract}
